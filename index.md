Privacy Policy for LockedIn
​Effective Date: August 17, 2026

​LockedIn is dedicated to helping you achieve discipline and build positive habits. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use the LockedIn mobile application.
​1. Information We Collect
​LockedIn is designed with privacy at its core. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on external servers. All core functionality runs entirely on your device.
​A. Data Stored Locally
​Habit & Goal Tracking Data: Your habit schedules, goals, streaks, check-in logs, and custom rules are stored locally on your device.
​Preferences & App Lock Rules: Application blocking configurations, strict mode preferences, and onboard settings are saved locally on your device.
​B. Android System Permissions
​To provide app-blocking and habit-enforcement features, LockedIn requests specific system permissions:
​Accessibility Services (AppLockAccessibilityService): Used solely to detect when a blocked or restricted application is opened in the foreground, allowing LockedIn to display the lock screen overlay.
​Usage Stats Permission (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Used locally to monitor screen time and evaluate app usage rules.
​System Overlay / Display Over Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Used to display the lock overlay screen when restricted applications are launched.
​Notification Access: Used to deliver local reminders and habit check-in prompts.
​Note: Accessibility Services and Usage Stats are used strictly on-device for local app-blocking functions and are never used to read personal messages, monitor typing, collect passwords, or track web browsing activity.
​2. How We Use Your Data
​All data processed by LockedIn is strictly used to provide, maintain, and optimize application features locally:
​Managing habit completion status, streaks, and statistics.
​Enforcing custom app-blocking rules and strict mode locks.
​Generating localized reminders and notifications.
​3. Data Sharing and Third Parties
​No Data Selling or Sharing: We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any personal information with third parties.
​No Remote Analytics or Tracking: We do not transmit tracking metrics or behavioral data to external analytics services.
​4. Data Retention and Deletion
​Because all your data is stored locally on your device:
​Manual Erasure: You can clear all data at any time by navigating to your device's settings: Settings > Apps > LockedIn > Storage > Clear Data.
​App Uninstallation: Uninstalling the LockedIn application will permanently delete all local databases, app-blocking rules, and habit logs from your device.
​5. Security
​We prioritize the protection of your local data. LockedIn relies on Android’s sandboxed security model to keep your local database and preferences isolated from other applications on your device.
​6. Children's Privacy
​LockedIn is not intended to collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Because the app does not collect or transmit personal information off the device, no information from children is knowingly gathered or stored.
​7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
​We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time as new features are added. Any updates will be reflected within the app or repository documentation with a revised effective date.
